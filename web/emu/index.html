Niagara
Drift

Drone synthesizer firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1.

Two drifting virtual-analog drones, each with a partner, a sub and a resonant filter, through delay and reverb. Inspired by the Stylophone DS-2, Subharmonicon and Lyra-8, and the amplified 1960s drones of La Monte Young and Angus MacLise.

Niagara Drift is free, open-source firmware (GPL‑3.0) for the M-VAVE FM‑1, made by Evan Yerburgh; not made or endorsed by M-VAVE. Source on GitHub

Named for Day of Niagara (the Theatre of Eternal Music, 1965) and La Monte Young’s Drift Studies. As an instrument it’s inspired by the Stylophone CPM DS-2, the Moog Subharmonicon and the Soma Lyra‑8.

Built on FoMni and X0X by Charles Vestal, and on Felucca’s FM‑1 platform by Leo Kuroshita (Hügelton Instruments), all GPL‑3.0. This page is set in Inter Tight, IBM Plex Mono and Barlow Semi Condensed (SIL OFL). Licensing and credits

This is the firmware itself running at the FM‑1’s 44.1 kHz, not a recording; SAVE keeps your sound in this browser. The idea of running FM‑1 firmware in a browser comes from Groove OS.
