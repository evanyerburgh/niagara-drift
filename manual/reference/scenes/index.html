The white keys play your saved sounds, gliding in, or crossfading as you press.

Scenes

White keys 1–8 hold scenes: saved sounds you move between.

What a scene keeps. The sound and its Tuning, but not the room: Pressure, Stereo, Tune, Sync, Pitch lock and the Setup times stay as they are.

Tap a new scene key

Glides to the scene, and stays.

Press a new scene key

Crossfades toward the scene, as far as you press. Let go and it glides back.

Keep a blend

Tap SEL while pressing: the blend stays.

Take it

Press all the way and hold a moment: the scene stays when you let go.

Undo

SEL + SAVE: back to the sound before. Again: forward.

Scene banks

Eight banks of eight scenes: banks 1 to 6 are yours, and two hold the presets. The screen names your scenes by bank and key, 3.5 for bank 3, key 5, and the presets by number, P5.

Save a scene

Hold SAVE, tap a key.

SAVE first. Hold SAVE before you touch the key: a key pressed first plays its scene instead. The presets can't be saved over.

Pick a bank

Hold SEL and tap a gesture key. The bank's scenes are then on white keys 1–8.

Good to know

Playing scenes

  • The glide takes the Scene time (Setup 1). Turn a knob to stop it part way.
  • A tap is anything let go within half a second, however hard.
  • The screen says how far a press has gone ("3.5 40%"), "KEPT" for a kept blend, and "→ 3.5" as it glides back.
  • The keys' lights: lit keys hold a scene; the one you're on blinks slowly until you change something.
  • Keys 9–16 are the gestures.

From 0.2

  • From 0.2: scenes on keys 9–12 moved to empty keys 1–8, their own bank's first, then banks 4 and 5 if needed (the screen says how many); none is written over. 0.2's presets that 0.3 doesn't keep (Eerie, Glass, Tanpura, Whistle, Cathedral, Metal) are in bank 6, keys 1–6 (6.1 to 6.6); Heaven is now Sibyl, Clean is Bare. Feedback moved to KNOB 1 on Life, Cross-mod to KNOB 4 on Mods, Tune to KNOB 2 on Setup 2; HOME opens Drones, FX opens Space.
