The 11 black keys are switches, with two sets of jobs: one for the drones, on every page but the LFO pages, and one for the LFO, while an LFO page is open. LFO reset (D#4) and X-mod (F#5) do the same in both.

Drone keys

On every other page, each drone has three keys, and five more are for both.

Each drone's own keys:

SwitchDrone IDrone IIChoices
PartnerF#3F#4On or off
SubG#3G#4−1 oct, −2 oct, ⅓, ⅕, −3 oct, −4 oct
FilterA#3A#4Low-, band- or high-pass (on screen LP, BP, HP)

The keys for both:

KeySwitchWhat it does
C#4Pitch lockScenes and presets keep the drones' pitches (on to start)
D#4LFO resetRestarts the LFO; hold to lock it to the Swell
C#5Pitch linkDrone II follows drone I
D#5Hard syncDrone II syncs to drone I
F#5X-modCross-mod's mode: off; cutoff (I moves II's cutoff); pitch (the drones bend each other's pitch); snarl

LFO keys

On the LFO pages, each drone's four keys set its two sends.

Each drone's own keys:

SwitchDrone IDrone IIChoices
Pitch LFO (on screen Osc LFO)F#3F#4Which LFO moves the pitch: I, II or both
Pitch to X-Wave (Osc to)G#3G#4The pitch send moves X-Wave instead
Cutoff LFO (Filt LFO)A#3A#4Which LFO moves the cutoff: I, II or both
Cutoff to reso (Filt to)C#4C#5The cutoff send moves resonance instead

The others:

KeySwitchChoices
D#5Delay LFO (Dly LFO)Which LFO moves the delay's time: I, II or both
D#4 · F#5LFO reset, X-modAs on every other page

Finding a key while you play

SEL + any black key shows what the key does, without switching it.

  1. Hold SEL. The screen draws the black keys, lit as on the panel.
  2. Still holding SEL, press a black key. The screen names it and its setting; nothing changes.
  3. Let go of SEL, then tap the key to switch it.

Good to know

Lights: dark is off, lit is on. A key of several choices shows which by its light:

KeyDimLitWinking

Pitch to X-Wave, Cutoff to resoPitch, CutoffX-Wave, Reso

  • An LFO key switched: the screen says what now moves what ("LFO II → I PITCH").
  • Pitch lock: the pitch knobs still tune.
  • Pitch link: KNOB 3 on Pitch sets the gap ("+7 st").
  • LFO reset: hold half a second to lock the LFO to the Swell; hold again to free it.
  • A key the screen names in a box ("needs [F#3]") blinks while the message shows.
