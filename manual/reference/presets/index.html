16 sounds built into Niagara Drift, each showing a different side of it and changing over a minute. They sit in two banks of eight on white keys 1–8.

P1–P8

Hold SEL and tap F5 for this bank, then tap white keys 1–8.

KeyPresetWhat it shows
P1NiagaraThe starting sound: D and A a fifth apart, breathing slowly
P2EmbersD and a pure third above, triangles blooming toward saws and back, the two drones in turn, a chorus hall
P3VespersSaws through band-passes near a sung "ah", the waves turning slowly, a gentle Swell (tremolo), a bright cathedral
P4DreamhouseOn the Harmonics Tuning: A1 and its 7th harmonic, sines with pure fifths, the resonance swelling, a little feedback
P5AbyssDark: D over B a minor third below, a saw and a narrow square, a subharmonic sub, closed resonant filters, character and feedback, a dark hall; the resonance swells and rings, and the Swell sinks it all slowly, like a tide
P6SeanceOn Harrison's Tuning: a hollow bass (D with a fifth under it) under a high band-passed voice with a breath of noise; drone I moving drone II's filter, long swinging echoes, a chorus hall; the high voice wanders, rarely
P7SonarDrone I slowed to 2.7 Hz, a click each cycle pinging through the delay, drone II's resonant filter singing as it sweeps
P8SibylA voice: D4 and F♯5, sines with just thirds and fifths, each wave turning into an "ah", a gentle Swell, a bright cathedral

P9–P16

Hold SEL and tap G5 for this bank, then tap white keys 1–8.

KeyPresetWhat it shows
P9HowlLow, D and E♭ a semitone apart so they grind, the feedback high, drone II band-passed; a random LFO flares the filters every few seconds
P10CisternReeds in a cistern: a fifth apart, drone I rounder with a sine sub, a bellows Swell, a tape delay a second long, the Shimmer reverb
P11PistonThe low D stepping a whole tone up and back about once a second, driving drone II's filter; a slow Swell
P12AshramA warm organ on E♭ on the Shruti Tuning: a filtered pulse over a sine sub, a second voice about 24 cents sharp, the bass wandering in Dorian, a chorus hall
P13TelegraphA stepped figure on drone II's pitch and filter over a low D; every 20 seconds or so the whole figure moves to a new note
P14TapeWorn: D and F, a wide square and a saw, Age and lots of Character, tape wow, a tape delay, hiss, a slow tired Swell
P15FallingOn the LMY Tuning: sines on E4 and G3, the filters opening slowly, a falling, gliding top, feedback, the bass wandering slowly
P16BareOne oscillator, everything else off: a plain saw to build from. Drone II is out: SEL + PLAY brings it in

Good to know

  • Pitch lock (C#4) is on when a new FM-1 starts: the presets keep the pitch you're on. Turn it off to hear each at its own notes. A preset's Tuning comes with it either way.
  • They're fixed: to keep your changes, save them in a bank of your own, 1 to 6 (recipe).
  • Most are rooted on D, so moving between them keeps the root.
  • Their loudest moments match P1's; Bare is quieter on purpose.
