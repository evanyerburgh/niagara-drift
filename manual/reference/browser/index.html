Computer keys

A key held down holds the control, as a finger would.

KeysOn the FM-1
1 … 0, −Black keys 1–11
Q … IScene keys 1–8
A … F · G … KGesture keys 9–12 · 13–16
Space · Return · EscPLAY · SAVE · HOME
C · V · Z · XSEL · EDIT · OCT− · OCT+
↑ ↓ · ← →LFO rate (SELECT) · Movement (ALGORITHM)
Tab, then the arrowsAny knob, one at a time

Good to know

  • No pressure in a browser: a scene key only taps (glides to its scene); holding it doesn't crossfade. A gesture key 9–12 held climbs all the way at the gesture's own time, and falls when you let go. On the FM-1 those keys follow how hard you press.
  • SAVE keeps your sound in that browser.
  • Working on the code: ./emulator.command builds the same page from your checkout and serves it at http://localhost:8765.
