A tremolo on both drones, from a slow breath to a hard gate, on its own or in time with a clock.

ARP opens the Swell page.

Controls

On the page, tap ARP to start or stop it.

KnobWhat it does
RateHow fast it breathes. On a MIDI clock it's Division: 32 bars to a quarter note
DepthHow far it dips, down to −30 dB; all the way, silence
ShapeEven (a smooth breath) in the middle; a ramp to the right; a pulse, then a hard gate, to the left
SwingEvery other breath longer, up to 3 to 1

Good to know

  • The starting sound has it on: a slow breath, 12 dB deep. The header says on, or off · tap ARP.
  • "ramp 100": the rise takes 97% of each breath.
  • An LFO locked to the Swell (D#4, held) restarts on its bars, so it follows a clock too.
  • The picture: two bars, four breaths, as strands meeting at a dot where the Swell is now.
