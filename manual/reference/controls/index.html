The FM-1's knobs and buttons, and the screen header at the top of the screen.

Knobs

KnobWhat it does
MASTERThe volume. Past about four-fifths it overdrives: thicker, at about the same loudness
SELECTThe LFO's rate
ALGORITHMMovement: how much the LFO moves the sound
PRESETSGesture time: how fast the gestures move
KNOB 1–4What the page shows. On the drone pages, KNOB 1–2 are drone I, KNOB 3–4 drone II

Buttons

ButtonWhat it does
HOME FX ENV LFO EDIT SEQ GLO ARPOpen their pages. HOME always opens Drones and FX Space; the others open the page you last used there. Tap again for its other page
PLAYFades the drones in or out
SAVESaves the sound
SELHold for fine steps, and with other controls for their second job
RECRecords your moves, then loops them
OCT− OCT+LFO I's and LFO II's shape

Screen header

SideWhat it shows
LeftThe page's name, then what's running: REC or LOOP, SWELL, a gesture, II OUT
RightThe sound: its bank and key ("3.5", "P6 SEANCE"), "changed" once you've moved it

Good to know

Pages and the screen

  • Hold a page button to peek: turn a knob, let go, and you're back ("let go: DRONES").
  • HOME and FX always open Drones and Space; the other buttons open the page you last used there.
  • The drone pages: Drones, Pitch, Tone, Osc, Sub · Drive, Partner, LFO sends: each shows drone I and drone II in their own colors.
  • On the way to a scene the header says where, in gold ("→ P3 VESPERS"), a gold line filling under it.

Buttons

  • SEL + PLAY fades drone II alone. Stopped, PLAY breathes slowly, and nothing else on the panel moves.
  • SAVE saves at once (the sound dips for about 50 ms while playing), and glows dimly while something isn't saved.
  • SEL gives a pitch knob single cents, cutoff and resonance tenths. It glows when it can keep what you're holding.
  • REC: tap to record, tap to loop, tap to stop; up to 5 minutes, the time in the header. See the recipe.
  • OCT− / OCT+: tap for the next shape; SEL + OCT for the one before. Each light pulses with its wave.

Knobs

  • Every knob glides: a slow turn is a smooth sweep, a fast turn goes further.
  • Overdriving MASTER flattens the drones' swells and dips.
