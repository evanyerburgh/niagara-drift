Notes tune the drones and a clock puts them in time, over USB or the TRS jack.

Messages

MessageWhat it does
Notes, channel 1Tune drone I to the note
Notes, channel 2Tune drone II; with Pitch link on, set the interval
ClockPuts the drones in time: after about two beats it locks ("120 BPM")
StartBrings the Swell back to its top at once; the LFO glides back to its own
Stop, ContinueIgnored: when the clock stops coming, everything carries on at the last tempo ("keeps 120")

Sync

Setup 1, KNOB 4: On follows a clock, Off ignores it. Under it, what the clock is doing:

Under SyncMeans
120 BPMFollowing a clock at that tempo
keeps 120The clock stopped; the last tempo carries on
no clockSync is on, but nothing is coming in
free timeSync is off: the drones keep their own time

On a clock

What follows the clock, with Sync on:

WhatOn the clock
The Swell (KNOB 1 on its page)A breath a Division: 32 bars to 1/4
The LFO (SELECT)A cycle a division: 32 bars to 1/16 ("LFO CYCLE")
The delay (Time, Details)A note value: 1/16, 1/8T, 1/8, 1/4T, 1/8D, 1/4, 1/2T, 1/4D, 1/2, 1/2D, 1 bar
WanderIts notes land on the beat (on the bar, when slow), still at random
Scene time (Setup 1)In bars: 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16; a scene change ends on a bar line

Good to know

Recipe: Play in time with a MIDI clock

Notes

  • Notes tune, even with Pitch lock on; they don't start or stop the drones. Note-offs, velocity, other channels and controllers (CC) are ignored.

Clock

  • Sync off ignores the clock and Start; notes still tune.
  • The clock needs to be at least 20 BPM.
  • A wobbly clock is smoothed, and nothing drifts from it however long it runs.
  • While synced, scenes leave the Swell's on / off and division alone.
  • A delay note value longer than the delay holds is halved.
  • In Logic: File → Project Settings → Synchronization → MIDI, tick Clock for the FM-1.

What goes out

  • One thing goes out: over USB, the FM-1 sends how hard you press each pressure key (scene keys 1–8 and keys 9–12) as polyphonic aftertouch on channel 1, on that key's own note. A DAW can record it, or let it move another synth. It sends no notes and no clock.
