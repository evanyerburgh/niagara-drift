A second oscillator for each drone, and how the drones' pitches move: Shift, glide and Wander.

Knobs

SEQ opens Partner; SEQ again, Pitch moves.

Partner

KNOB 1KNOB 2KNOB 3KNOB 4
I intervalI detuneII intervalII detune
KnobWhat it does
IntervalFrom an octave below to an octave and a fifth above, each tempered or just (a pure ratio that doesn't beat)
DetuneUp to 30 cents, so the two beat

On and off

A black key switches each partner: F#3 for drone I, F#4 for drone II.

Pitch moves

KNOB 1KNOB 2KNOB 3KNOB 4
Shift's movePitch glideII wanderScale
KnobWhat it does
Shift's moveWhere the Shift gesture (E5) takes the drones: a fifth down to a fifth up
Pitch glide (on screen Glide)How long a drone takes to reach a new pitch: up to 10 s
WanderA slow random melody for drone II on the Scale. The knob is how often; 0 is off
ScaleFor Wander and the Chord gesture: minor pentatonic, major pentatonic, fifths and octaves, whole tone, Dorian, or just

Good to know

Partner

  • Just intervals don't beat; Detune adds beating on purpose. On the other Tunings, intervals take the Tuning's own, named in its terms ("+11\19", "+3/2"); see Intervals on a Tuning.
  • A partner off: Detune goes gray ("needs [F#3]") and the key blinks while you turn it.

Pitch moves

  • Shift's move goes a note of the Tuning a click ("+5th", "+m3"); with SEL, a cent a click.
  • Wander stays within an octave of drone II's pitch and comes home now and then: 25–30 s apart at the bottom of the knob, 0.5–2 s at the top. Pitch glide slides its notes.
  • Just (on the Scale) is drone I's harmonics 1–8. The Scale's notes follow the Tuning.

Pictures

  • On Partner, each drone and its partner as a knot that rolls as they beat ("beats 2.0/s"). On Pitch moves, Shift's move on the left and drone II's last four Wander notes on the right.
