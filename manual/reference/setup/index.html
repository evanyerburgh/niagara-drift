Settings for the FM-1 itself, not for a sound: how long changes take, the clock, the Tuning and the keys' touch.

Knobs

GLO opens Setup 1; GLO again, Setup 2. These belong to the FM-1, not to a sound: scenes don't change them, except the Tuning.

Tunings

A sound carries its Tuning. A scene saved on LMY brings LMY back, even with Pitch lock on. A preset plays on 12-tone unless it has its own. The Tuning changes half way through a scene's glide.

TuningWhat it is
12-toneSemitones
QuarterQuarter tones
19-, 31-, 53-toneEqual temperaments
JustJust intonation (Ptolemy's 5-limit)
HarmonicsThe odd harmonics 1–15 of A: the drones as notes of one harmonic series
LMYLa Monte Young's tuning for The Well-Tuned Piano
HarrisonLou Harrison's tuning for his National Steel guitar
Shrutis (on screen Shruti)The 22 shrutis of Indian music
PartchHarry Partch's 43 notes to the octave

Intervals on a Tuning

Named as composers write them: steps on the equal ones ("11\19"), ratios on the others ("3/2", "7/4"), when what you hear is within 10 cents; otherwise the familiar name ("5th").

Good to know

Knobs

  • The time knobs give most of their turn to slow times, a second about a quarter of the way.
  • Sync shows the clock under it: "no clock", "120 BPM", "keeps 120" (stopped, at the last tempo) or "free time".
  • Tune moves 1 Hz a click, 0.1 with SEL ("432Hz").
  • A drone playing alone sits in the middle, whatever Stereo says.
  • The speaker gets a low cut (about 120 Hz) for bass it can't play; it's off while headphones are in.

Tunings

  • Just and Shruti move with drone I (a movable do): its pitch knob picks the tonic. The others are built on A, so Tune moves them; LMY's 1/1 is C, Harrison's and Partch's G.
  • Wander, Chord, a partner's interval and Shift take the Tuning's notes.

Pictures

  • On Setup 1, a row for each time, ending where its knob is; on Setup 2, the Tuning as a ring, a tick a note, turned by Tune.
