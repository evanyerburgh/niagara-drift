Time and transitions

Fast cuts or slow glides: time your transitions, and stop them part way

SoundsGesturesScenes and savingTime and transitionsPerformingFrom scratch

Three times on Setup 1 (GLO): Fade (KNOB 1), Morph (KNOB 2) and Scene time (KNOB 3).

All 4 recipes on this page
  • Cut or glide between scenes Stop a glide part way Make a switch move slowly Fade in slowly, or fade one drone out

Cut or glide between scenes

  1. Press GLO until you're on Setup 1.
  2. Turn Scene time (KNOB 3) all the way left.
  3. Tap a different scene key: the sound cuts straight to it.
  4. Turn Scene time up, then tap another scene key: the sound glides there, taking as long as Scene time says.

Listen

  1. P1 Niagara, Scene time 0.1 s
  2. P5 Abyss tapped: a cut
  3. P1 tapped: a cut back
  4. Scene time 8 s, P5 tapped: a slow glide

Stop a glide part way

  1. Tap a scene on a slow Scene time.
  2. Turn any knob on the way: the glide stops there, a sound between the two.

Listen

  1. P1 Niagara, Scene time 8 s
  2. P5 Abyss tapped: a slow glide
  3. A knob turned: the glide stops there

Make a switch move slowly

  1. Turn Morph up.
  2. Tap a black key, or an LFO's shape (OCT− or OCT+): its change comes in over that time.

Listen

  1. P3 Vespers, Morph 0.02 s
  2. A#3 tapped: thin and bright, at once
  3. Morph 8 s, tapped again: the body comes back slowly
  4. Again: it thins as slowly

Fade in slowly, or fade one drone out

  1. Turn Fade up, then tap PLAY: the drones come in over that time.
  2. Hold SEL and tap PLAY: drone II alone fades out (again, and it comes back).

Listen

  1. P13 Telegraph, Fade 10 s, PLAY tapped: in over 10 s
  2. SEL + PLAY: drone II fades out
