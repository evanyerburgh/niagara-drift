Tone and texture

Ticks and clicks

Put drone I's sub four octaves down, all the way in, and turn drone I down to about 21 Hz: the sub stops being a tone and becomes clicks, about 2.6 a second. Turn drone I down and the clicks play like percussion under drone II.

  1. Hold SEL and tap F5 for the presets, then tap white key 2: Embers (P2).
  2. Press EDIT until you're on Sub · Drive, and turn KNOB 1, Osc/Sb, all the way to the sub: 100.
  3. Tap G#3, drone I's sub, until the screen says −4 oct. It goes round −1, −2, ⅓, ⅕, −3, −4.
  4. Press HOME until you're on Pitch, and turn KNOB 1, drone I's pitch, quickly down past A0, where the screen turns to Hz, to about 21 Hz: clicks.
  5. Press HOME until you're on Drones, and turn KNOB 1, drone I's level, down to about 50: the clicks under drone II.
Good to know

The clicks a second are drone I's pitch ÷ 8 (the sub is ÷ 16, two clicks to its cycle): at D3, 18 a second; at A0 (27.5 Hz), about 3; at 21 Hz, 2.6; at 5 Hz, one every 1.6 s. The clicks reach the speaker, though the hum under them doesn't. The Tick gesture (G4) does this on its own, faster as you press harder. Or take drone I itself under 20 Hz with Osc/Sb at 0: a click each cycle, as in Sonar (P7).

Listen

  1. Embers (P2) as it starts: no clicks
  2. Osc/Sb to the sub, the sub to −4 oct, drone I's pitch quickly down to about 21 Hz, its level down to 50
  3. Clicks, about 2.6 a second, as percussion under drone II's blooming triangle

A singing filter

Turn a filter's resonance up and it rings on the drone's notes; let the LFO move the resonance instead of the cutoff, and the ring swells and fades on its own.

  1. Press HOME until you're on Pitch, and turn KNOB 2 and KNOB 4, the resonances, up: the filters ring. Their last dot is red: there they ring on their own.
  2. Press HOME until you're on Drones, and turn the cutoffs (KNOB 2 and KNOB 4) up: the ring brighter.
  3. Press LFO until you're on LFO sends, and tap C#4 and C#5: on the LFO pages they switch each drone's cutoff send to resonance.
  4. Turn KNOB 2 and KNOB 4, the resonance sends, up: the ring swells and fades as the LFO goes round.
Good to know

Abyss (P5) swells its resonances this way. With the drones a fifth or an octave apart the two rings meet; a semitone apart, they beat. Weight (D5) lets the resonance give way while you hold it.

Listen

  1. P15 Falling, both resonances up and both cutoffs opened: the filters ring on the drones' notes
  2. The filter LFOs moved to resonance: the ring swells and fades, slowly, again and again

Hard sync sweep

With hard sync, drone II restarts its wave on every cycle of drone I: turning drone II's pitch then changes its tone, not its note, a tearing, vocal, laser-like sweep.

  1. Press HOME until you're on Drones: turn KNOB 4, drone II's cutoff, well up, and bring drone II forward (KNOB 3 up, KNOB 1 down).
  2. Tap black key 10, D#5: hard sync, drone II locked to drone I.
  3. Press HOME until you're on Pitch, and turn KNOB 3, drone II's pitch, slowly up two octaves and back: the tone sweeps.
Good to know

It's strongest with drone II above drone I and a saw or square in its wave (Osc page). To keep it moving without a hand, send the LFO to drone II's pitch (LFO sends, KNOB 3): a send of about 30 moves it two semitones each way, a gentle sweep; 100 swings it two octaves. Here Abyss's own resonance LFO was turned off first (LFO sends, KNOB 2 and 4 to 0), so its slow ringing doesn't cover the sweep.

Listen

  1. P5 Abyss, drone II opened and brought forward
  2. Hard sync on: a harder, buzzier edge
  3. Drone II's pitch turned slowly up two octaves: its tone tears upward, a vocal, laser-like sweep
  4. And back down: the sweep falls
  5. Up again, faster

Cross-mod

Drone I shaping drone II

At key 11's first setting, drone I's sound moves drone II's filter, as fast as drone I sounds: drone II growls, buzzes and takes on drone I's pitch as a grain.

  1. Tap black key 11, F#5, once: it lights, I moves II's cutoff.
  2. Press LFO until you're on Mods, and turn KNOB 4, X-mod, up: the growl deepens.
  3. Press HOME until you're on Pitch, and move drone I's pitch (KNOB 1): the growl changes its grain.
Good to know

It's strongest with drone II's filter resonant and band-pass (A#4), as in Vespers (P3) here. Seance (P6) uses it gently.

Listen

  1. P3 Vespers as it starts: saws through band-pass filters
  2. Key 11 once: drone I's sound moves drone II's filter: drone II growls and shimmers
  3. X-mod up: the growl deepens into a buzzing, vocal edge
  4. Drone I's pitch moved: the growl changes its grain

Metallic bells

Each drone's sound bends the other's pitch, FM synthesis between the two. On pure, high tones a tritone apart it rings into a metallic tone. A bell is struck and dies away, so the LFO snaps the filters open and lets them close slowly: a bell struck every 2.5 s, ringing out.

  1. Press EDIT until you're on Osc, and turn KNOB 1 and KNOB 3, the X-Waves, all the way down: sine.
  2. Press HOME until you're on Pitch, and turn both drones high, a tritone apart (here C#6 and G6: the top of their range is about 1.6 kHz).
  3. Tap black key 11, F#5, until it winks and the screen says PITCH I <> II: the drones bend each other's pitch (twice from off).
  4. Press LFO until you're on Mods, and turn KNOB 4, X-mod, up to about 70: the sines ring into metal.
  5. Press HOME until you're on Drones, and turn both cutoffs (KNOB 2 and KNOB 4) down to about 300 Hz: the metal goes dull.
  6. Press LFO until you're on LFO sends. Tap OCT− until LFO I is Round (Roundtooth), and turn SELECT, the LFO's rate, to about 0.4 Hz. Tap A#4 until drone II's cutoff says LFO I too.
  7. Turn KNOB 2 and KNOB 4, the cutoff sends, all the way left: −100. Each cycle the filters snap open and close slowly: the bell is struck, and rings out.
  8. Press FX until you're on Space, and turn KNOB 4, the reverb's Mix, to about 40: more room for it to ring in.
Good to know

Roundtooth rises fast and falls at the cycle's end; turned upside down (−100) it jumps and dies away, a strike. SELECT sets how often it strikes. Simple intervals (an octave, a fifth) lock into harmonies instead of bells; awkward ones (a tritone, a seventh) ring metallic. X-mod past 85 clangs and runs wild. If the Swell is on, switch it off (ARP, then ARP again on its page) so only the LFO strikes.

Listen

  1. A bell struck every 2.5 s (0:03, 0:05, 0:08, 0:10, 0:13): bright and clangy on the hit
  2. The top dies first, and it fades about 25 dB into a dark hum before the next

Snarl

At key 11's last setting, the finished sound, the delay's echoes included, bends both drones' pitch: a growl that drags and tears the pitch, and the echoes with it. It's clearest on a steady sound with plenty of echoes.

  1. Press FX until you're on Space, and turn Repeat (KNOB 1) and Mix (KNOB 2) up: more echoes for Snarl to drag.
  2. Tap black key 11, F#5, three times from off: Snarl (it winks).
  3. Press LFO until you're on Mods, and turn KNOB 4, X-mod, up: 40 a growl, 85 a snarl.
Good to know

The Snarl gesture (B4) brings it in by pressure, with the drive and Feedback on top, and leaves without a trace. Clean (F5) takes it out while you hold it.

Listen

  1. P2 Embers with more echoes: steady triangles blooming toward saws
  2. Snarl on: the finished sound starts to bend both drones
  3. X-mod up: the snarl grows, the pitch dragged and torn, the echoes bending with it

Movement and rhythm

A stepped figure that moves

LFO II's OR shape adds two square waves, one five times as fast as the other: on a drone's pitch it steps round four notes, a figure that repeats. Wander then moves the whole figure to a new note now and then.

  1. Make drone II bright, so the figure stands out: press EDIT until you're on Osc and turn KNOB 3, its wave, to a saw; then on Drones (HOME) turn KNOB 4, its cutoff, all the way up, KNOB 3, its level, up, and KNOB 1, drone I's level, down.
  2. Press LFO until you're on LFO sends, and tap OCT+, LFO II's wave, until the screen says OR 5.
  3. Turn KNOB 3, drone II's pitch send, up: 40 is about ±4 semitones.
  4. Turn SELECT, the LFO's rate, up for a quicker figure.
  5. Press SEQ until you're on Pitch moves, and turn KNOB 3, Wander, up: every so often the figure starts on another note.
Good to know

LFO I's OR (OCT−) is three times as fast: another figure. AND (AND 3 on LFO I, AND 5 on LFO II) steps between two notes instead, as in Piston (P11). Telegraph (P13) is built this way, on LFO I's OR 3.

Listen

  1. The figure stepping: drone II bright, round four notes on LFO II's OR 5
  2. Wander up: every few seconds the whole figure moves to a new note

A filter rhythm in time with Swell

The Swell makes both drones breathe; lock the LFO to it and a square wave on the filters jumps on the same beat, the two filters in turn.

  1. Press ARP for the Swell page (tap it again if the Swell is off), and turn KNOB 1 for about a breath a second.
  2. Press LFO until you're on LFO sends. Tap OCT− until LFO I is Square, and tap key 8, A#4, until LFO I moves drone II's cutoff too.
  3. Turn KNOB 2 and KNOB 4, the cutoff sends, one up and one down: the filters jump in turn. Turn SELECT to about the Swell's speed.
  4. Hold black key 5, D#4, half a second: the LFO restarts every two of the Swell's breaths, a bar ("on Swell").
Good to know

Echoes blur the rhythm: if your sound has them, turn the delay's Mix down first (FX, KNOB 2). The Swell's speed sets the rhythm: the locked LFO restarts every bar, two breaths. Hold D#4 again to free the LFO. (With a MIDI clock, the Swell and the locked LFO follow its beat too.)

Listen

  1. P8 Sibyl, its delay off and its Swell quick: the drones breathe about once a second
  2. LFO I to Square on both cutoffs: the filters jump in turn
  3. The LFO locked to the Swell: each jump lands on its breath

LFO chasing itself

Speed mod lets the LFO speed up and slow down on one of its own waves, so every wobble rushes, then drags. It's easiest to hear on a vibrato.

  1. Press LFO until you're on LFO sends. Tap key 6, F#4, until LFO I moves drone II's pitch, and turn KNOB 3, its pitch send, up: a slow vibrato.
  2. Press LFO until you're on Mods, and turn KNOB 1, Speed, all the way up: the LFO speeds up and slows down by up to three octaves.
  3. Turn KNOB 2, Source, to LFO I: a regular rush and drag (LFO II's random glide makes it uneven).
Good to know

Speed mod moves the rate of everything the LFO moves at once: the filters, the pitches, the delay. Speed at 0 steadies it again.

Listen

  1. P7 Sonar with a vibrato on drone II: a slow, steady wobble
  2. Speed mod to the top, driven by LFO I's triangle: the wobble rushes, then drags, over and over
  3. Speed back to 0: the wobble steady again

Echoes and space

Echoes that warble

When the delay's time moves, its echoes bend in pitch, as tape does when it speeds up and slows down. Let an LFO move it, and every echo warbles; let the Swell dip the drones, and the bending echoes ring on their own in the gaps.

  1. Press FX until you're on Space, and turn Repeat (KNOB 1) and Mix (KNOB 2) up: clear echoes to bend.
  2. Press ARP for the Swell (tap it again if it's off), and turn KNOB 1 up for about a breath a second: the echoes sound in the gaps.
  3. Press LFO until you're on Mods, and turn KNOB 3, Delay, all the way up: the LFO wobbles the delay's time by a quarter, and every echo bends.
  4. Press FX until you're on Details, and turn Time (KNOB 2) up: deeper, slower bends.
Good to know

On the LFO pages, black key 10, D#5, picks which LFO moves the delay's time: LFO I's regular waves warble evenly, LFO II's random glide makes them wander, as here. Turning Time by hand bends the echoes too. Movement (ALGORITHM) scales the warble with the other LFO sends.

Listen

  1. P8 Sibyl with its echoes up and its Swell quick: the drones dip about once a second, the echoes ring in the gaps
  2. Delay all the way up: every echo bends in pitch, clearest in the gaps
  3. A longer Time: deeper, slower bends

Crackling echoes

The Self delay stretches its time as its echoes get louder, so loud echoes sag and wobble; Drive after it distorts the drones and their echoes together: ghost notes and crackle.

  1. Press FX until you're on Space, and turn Repeat (KNOB 1) up: the echoes pile up.
  2. Press FX until you're on Details. Hold SEL and turn KNOB 2 to Self (or Grain, whose own echoes shake its time).
  3. Turn KNOB 1, Drive, up.
Good to know

Past Repeat 85 the echoes run away into a ringing tone that holds itself ("runaway" in red); Self makes the runaway lurch. PLAY lets it ring out.

Listen

  1. P11 Piston as it starts
  2. Repeat up: the echoes pile up
  3. The delay to Self: its time stretching as the echoes get louder
  4. Drive up: the drones and their echoes distorted together, ghost notes and crackle

Freeze a chord, play over it

At its top, the reverb's Length is Inf: it never dies away, a freeze of what's in it. Move the drones and they play over the frozen chord.

  1. Press FX until you're on Space. Turn KNOB 4, the reverb's Mix, up, then KNOB 3, Length, all the way: Inf, "freeze" in blue.
  2. Press HOME until you're on Pitch, and turn KNOB 1 and KNOB 3 to new notes: the drones play over the frozen chord.
  3. Turn Length back down to let it go, or stop with PLAY.
Good to know

On Details, SEL + KNOB 4 picks what goes into the reverb: Drones freezes the drones and keeps the echoes dry on top. Damp is out at Inf, so the freeze holds what's in it.

Listen

  1. P10 Cistern as it starts
  2. Reverb Mix up, then Length to the top: the chord freezes and holds
  3. New notes for both drones: they play over the frozen chord

Shimmer on echoes

The Shimmer reverb lifts what goes into it an octave, in two voices just apart. Send it only the echoes, and each echo rises into a bright halo while the drones stay dry. It shines on voices in the middle and up; on a low drone the octave stays low.

  1. If your drones are low, press HOME until you're on Pitch and turn both up an octave (KNOB 1 and KNOB 3): the halo sparkles above them.
  2. Press FX until you're on Space, and turn Repeat (KNOB 1) and Mix (KNOB 2) up: more echoes.
  3. Press FX until you're on Details, and turn KNOB 4, the reverb's Type, to Shimmer.
  4. Hold SEL and turn KNOB 4: Into Echoes.
  5. Press FX until you're on Space, and turn the reverb's Mix (KNOB 4) up.
Good to know

The Shimmer washes in over about 8 seconds and out over 6. Cistern (P10) uses it on everything.

Listen

  1. P15 Falling with both drones an octave up (G4 and E5), its echoes up
  2. The reverb to Shimmer, Into Echoes: over 8 seconds the echoes rise an octave into a halo
