### Build a sound from scratch {#scratch}

*No preset needed: start bare, add what you like*

You don't have to start from a preset. **P16 Bare** is a plain analog synth: one saw through an open filter, everything else off. Build up from there, a control at a time, in any order.

#### Start bare

1. Hold [[SEL]] and tap [[G5]], tap white key 8 (**P16 Bare**), then [[PLAY]]. Wait for the gold line under
   [the header](../reference/controls.md#screen-header) to fill: Bare glides in over the scene time.

#### Add what you like

1. **Shape it:** on **Drones**, where you start, turn KNOB 2 down: drone I's cutoff darkens the saw.
2. **A second drone:** [[SEL]] + [[PLAY]] brings in drone II, a fifth above; on **Pitch** ([[HOME]]), KNOB 3 tunes it.
3. **Movement:** turn ALGORITHM up from 0%, then on **LFO sends** ([[LFO]]) turn KNOB 2 up: the filter breathes.
4. **Space:** on **Space** ([[FX]]), KNOB 2 for echoes, KNOB 4 for reverb.

#### Keep it

1. Hold [[SEL]] and tap [[G4]] for bank 1, then hold [[SAVE]] and tap a white key: your sound is stored there.
