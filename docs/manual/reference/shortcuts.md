## Shortcuts

Every combination of keys, and every hold and tap, in one place.

## With SEL

### Sounds and scenes

| Do | What it does |
|---|---|
| [[SEL]] + [[PLAY]] | Drone II alone fades out, or back in |
| [[SEL]] + [[SAVE]] | Back to the sound before your last scene tap or blend; again, forward. Keep SEL down until you've let go of SAVE |
| [[SEL]] + a gesture key | Picks the bank the scene keys play ([Scene banks](scenes.md#pick-a-bank)) |
| Tap [[SEL]] while holding a scene key | The blend stays where it is ("A5 40% KEPT") |
| Tap [[SEL]] while holding a gesture | It latches at the level it has reached |

### Knobs

| Do | What it does |
|---|---|
| [[SEL]] held, a knob | Fine steps: a pitch knob a cent at a time, cutoff and resonance in tenths, Shift's move a cent |
| [[SEL]] + KNOB 1 or 3 on Sub · Drive | Each drone's sub wave |
| [[SEL]] + KNOB 2 on Details | The delay's type |
| [[SEL]] + KNOB 4 on Details | What goes into the reverb |

### Keys and the LFO

| Do | What it does |
|---|---|
| [[SEL]] held | The black keys' map on the screen, the banks lit on the gesture keys |
| [[SEL]] held, a black key touched | The screen names it and its setting, without switching it |
| [[SEL]] + [[OCT−]] or [[OCT+]] | That LFO's shape before |

## Holds and taps

### Scene keys

| Do | What it does |
|---|---|
| Hold [[SAVE]], tap a white key | Save the sound on that key (not into the presets) |
| Press a white key harder | A crossfade into its scene, as far as you press |
| Press a white key all the way, a moment | The scene is taken: letting go keeps it |

### Gesture keys

| Do | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tap a gesture key | The gesture all the way in, latched; tap again and it goes |

### Buttons and pages

| Do | What it does |
|---|---|
| Hold a page button | Peek at its page: turn a knob, let go, and you're back |
| Tap a page button again | Its next page ("1/2") |
| Hold [[ARP]] | Peek at the Swell page |
| Tap [[ARP]] on the Swell page | The Swell on or off |
| Tap [[REC]] three times | Record your moves, loop them, stop |

### Black keys

| Do | What it does |
|---|---|
| Hold [[D#4]] half a second | The LFO locked to the Swell (again to free it) |

### A stuck install

| Do | What it does |
|---|---|
| Hold [[OCT−]] and [[OCT+]] for 5 seconds | The chip's own update mode, for a stuck install (see [Help](../help.md)) |
