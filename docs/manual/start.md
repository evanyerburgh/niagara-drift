The keys aren't notes: the drones play on their own. White keys 1–8 hold Presets and scenes, keys 9–12 and the top
four are gestures, the black keys are switches. The first time you do some things (blend into a scene, flip a black
key, play a gesture, open the effects…) the screen shows a one-line tip about what's next to it, once.

## From a Preset

Listen first, then change what you hear.

1. **[[PLAY]]:** the drones fade in.
2. **Tap the white keys 1–8:** a new FM-1 starts on the Presets, a sound on each key ([The Presets](reference/presets.md)).
3. **Hold a gesture key** and let go: a top key (Weight, Shift, Clean, Wash), or keys 9–12 (Tick, Chord, Snarl,
   Stutter), pressed harder for more ([The gestures](reference/gestures.md)).
4. **Play the drones:** Drones is the page you start on ([[HOME]] brings you back): each drone's level and cutoff,
   KNOB 1–4. [[HOME]] again for their pitch.
5. **Change the sound:** the page buttons ([[ENV]], [[EDIT]], [[LFO]], …) and KNOB 1–4 ([The controls](reference/controls.md)).
6. **Save your sound:** [[SEL]] + [[G4]] picks bank A, then hold [[SAVE]] and tap a white key.
7. **[[PLAY]] again:** the drones fade out.

## From Bare

Start from one plain oscillator, everything else off, and build a sound up a control at a time, as on an analog synth.

1. **Bare:** [[SEL]] + [[G5]] picks the second bank of Presets; tap white key 8 (P16 Bare) and [[PLAY]]. It glides
   there over the Scene time (about 8 seconds to start): wait for the gold line under the header to fill. One saw,
   drone I, through an open filter.
2. **The filter:** you start on Drones ([[HOME]] brings you back to it). Turn KNOB 2, drone I's cutoff, down until
   the saw darkens.
3. **Drone II:** [[SEL]] + [[PLAY]] brings it in, a fifth above. On Drones, KNOB 3 is its level and KNOB 4 its cutoff.
4. **The interval:** [[HOME]] again for Pitch. KNOB 3 moves drone II a note a click; the screen names the interval
   between them.
5. **The sub:** [[EDIT]] twice for Sub · Drive. KNOB 1 blends drone I toward its sub, an octave down.
6. **The LFO:** [[LFO]] opens LFO sends. Turn KNOB 2, drone I's cutoff send, up; then turn [[ALGORITHM]], Movement,
   up from 0% and the filter starts to breathe. [[SELECT]] sets how fast.
7. **Space:** [[FX]]. KNOB 2 brings in the delay's echoes, KNOB 4 the reverb.
8. **Life:** [[ENV]]. KNOB 2, Drift, lets the drones wander a little; KNOB 3, Character, softens them.
9. **Keep it:** [[SEL]] + [[G4]] picks bank A; hold [[SAVE]] and tap a white key.

Then try the [recipes](recipes/index.md).
