# fm1-drone: a drone player's review

Reviewed 2026-10-11 from `~/fm1-drone` on `main` (left unchanged). I played it through the host simulator and the browser emulator, recorded and measured the audio, and read the code. Every claim has a tag: **[tested]** means I ran it and measured or saw it, **[code]** means I read it in the source, **[opinion]** is my judgement as a player. Nothing has run on the real FM-1 yet, so anything about feel (detents, touch keys, how bright the screen is) is a prediction.

---

## 1. Summary: the five most important findings

1. **The basic idea works, and it suits long pieces.** KNOB 1–2 always control drone I and KNOB 3–4 always control drone II, and the home page puts pitch and cutoff for both drones under your hand. Every page is at most two presses away. Knobs glide, and switches move to their new setting over the Morph time: I flipped six switches during a recording and found no clicks **[tested]**. Movement (all LFO depths together on OCT−/OCT+), scenes that can morph over up to 5 minutes, and a 5-minute motion loop are real long-form tools that the DS-2 doesn't have. **[tested] / [opinion]**
2. **Tuning the harmony is the biggest musical gap.** A pitch knob moves 60 cents per click, with no snap to notes, so from D3 you can't reach C3 by turning (you land on 4820 or 4760 cents, not 4800). With pitch link on, the gap between the drones starts as an exact fifth (700 cents) but one click away there is no way back except by counting single cents. "Tuning: just" only applies to the partner oscillator, not to drone I against drone II, which is the interval a drone player cares about most. **[tested]**
3. **Three things can go wrong mid-performance.**
   - Pressing SAVE, or storing a scene, while the drone is playing cuts the audio to silence while the memory is written. This is roughly a 50–400 ms gap, and the cut is abrupt. **[code]**
   - Recalling a scene also brings back settings that belong to the room, not the sound: Speaker (the low cut), Tune and Pitch lock. **[tested]**
   - The low end goes subsonic: at the lowest pitch, the −2 octave and ⅕ subs put 59–90 % of the output's energy below 20 Hz, near full level, with no filter to stop it. **[tested]**
4. **A few pages are grab-bags, and one promised feature is missing.**
   - LFO 2 mixes four unrelated things (rate mod, a 3-way switch turned by a knob, the cross-mod amount, the delay LFO depth).
   - Pulse width sits away from X-Wave.
   - Two different settings are both called "Width".
   - The delay's LFO is split across three places.
   - The README promises a band-pass filter, but no control reaches it. **[code] / [tested]**
5. **Some hand and eye problems.**
   - The Swell page only appears while ARP is held, so it always takes two hands and you can't stay on it **[tested]**.
   - Fine pitch also takes two hands.
   - The screen labels are about 7 px tall, roughly 1 mm on a 1.4-inch screen **[tested]**: fine at the desk, hard to read standing up.
   - Three printed button names clash with what the buttons do; OCT− / OCT+ for Movement clashes most. **[opinion]**

## 2. Playability

**What works**
- **The home page is right.** Pitch I, cutoff I, pitch II and cutoff II are the four things you touch most in a drone, and they're all live with no menu **[code]**. LFO rate (SELECT) and both LFO shapes (PRESETS, ALGORITHM) are also always live on the left knobs, and the top bar shows their name and value as you turn **[tested]**: emu/04_wave1_turn.png.
- **The knobs feel continuous.** Each click slides over the time since the previous click, so a slow turn sweeps smoothly. The repo's own sweep test checks this, and my default-sound recording shows the slow filter LFO giving a gentle ±1 dB swell with no stepping **[tested]**: out/default.wav, level per second −9.7 to −11.8 dB.
- **Gestures for long pieces.** PLAY fades in or out over up to 60 s, a scene morphs over up to 5 minutes, and Movement scales all LFO depths from still to double. These are the kind of single gestures a 40-minute set needs **[code]**. Holding OCT+ took Movement from 100 % to 200 % in under 2 s **[tested]**: t8_move.
- **Feedback stays under control.** With Feedback, Repeats, Delay mix, Resonance and Drive all at maximum and MASTER fully up, the level crept from −6.3 to −4.7 dB RMS over 40 s and settled. It never clipped, and a howl near 1 kHz took over **[tested]**: out/extreme.wav. This is safe on stage, though some Lyra players will want it to be able to tip over **[opinion]**.

**What doesn't**
- **Two-handed holds.**
  - Fine pitch means holding SEL, which is in the button block, while turning KNOB 1 or 3.
  - The Swell is only editable while ARP is held, and letting go always returns you to the page you were on, so you can't park on the Swell page **[tested]**: t7_nav, `press ARP … release` leaves you on page 0.
  - You can never adjust a Swell setting and a drone setting together, and two Swell settings take both hands plus a third.
  - On a stand, in the dark, the "hold this, turn that" moves are the slowest part of the instrument **[opinion]**.
- **No way to drop out one drone.** PLAY fades both drones. To bring drone II in or out you have to go to Play 2 (HOME again) and turn its level by hand. The engine already has a per-drone fade (`dr_voice`) that nothing calls **[code]**. Bringing one voice in and out is a basic Lyra or shruti gesture **[opinion]**.
- **Saving during a performance.** SAVE and SAVE + white key write to memory, and that write silences the audio by zeroing the output buffer (`audio_silence` before every erase) **[code]**. Storing a scene in the middle of a piece, which is exactly when you'd want to, will make a hard gap. Autosave already waits until the drone is stopped, so that part is handled **[code]**.
- **HOME doesn't always take you home.** If you're already on Play, a tap of HOME goes to Play 2. I did this myself in a test script: I tapped HOME to "get home", and then my pitch turns went to resonance and level instead **[tested]**: first version of t4_lowend. Players will tap HOME as a safety move **[opinion]**.

## 3. Intuitiveness

**What a drone player will guess straight away**
- Colour coding: amber is drone I and cyan is drone II, on the screen, in the guide and in the key groups. It reads well on a dark stage **[tested]**: pages/montage.png.
- The pictures beat the numbers: oscilloscopes on Play, filter curves on Play 2, LFO routing lines on LFO, the delay taps, the swell shape. You can read them at arm's length even where the text is too small **[tested]**: pages/montage.png, emu/03_lfo_page.png.
- Pitch shows as a note name plus cents ("D3", "Eb3 −40"), which is exactly right for a drone **[code]**.
- Switches put up a banner naming what changed ("I OSC LFO WAVE II") **[tested]**: emu/05_switch_banner.png.

**What will confuse them**
- **Printed names against what the buttons do** **[opinion]**:

| Printed | Does | How much it clashes |
|---|---|---|
| HOME | Play / Play 2 | fine |
| FX, LFO | Effects, LFO | fine |
| ARP | Swell | acceptable (it's rhythmic) |
| SEQ | Partner | arbitrary, must be learned |
| ENV | Tone (drive, pulse width) | misleading: there are no envelopes |
| EDIT | Osc | arbitrary |
| GLO | Setup | close enough ("global") |
| **OCT− / OCT+** | **Movement** | **strong clash**: on a drone synth everyone will press OCT to change octave |

- **The black keys have two layers with no printed legend.** On the LFO pages the same 11 keys mean something else, and "lit" changes meaning: on the normal layer lit means on, on the LFO layer lit means Wave II and winking means both **[code]**. The screen shows the result as a banner, but nothing tells you before you press a key what it will do **[opinion]**.
- **Global keys sit inside the drone groups.** The keys come in groups of 3, 2, 3, 2, 1. Pitch lock (global) sits in drone I's oscillator group and LFO reset (global) in drone I's filter group **[code]**. The guide draws them inside the amber boxes, which suggests they belong to drone I **[tested]**: emu/10_guide.png.
- **Two settings called "Width".** Pulse width on Tone and stereo width on Setup both show as "Width" on the knob row **[tested]**: pages/montage.png. The guide works around it with "Width (stereo)".
- **The band-pass filter isn't there.** The README says "low-, band- or high-pass", but the key only toggles low-pass and high-pass. Band-pass exists in the engine and in the screen's drawing, but nothing turns it on **[code]**: ui.c line 350.
- **The on-page guide is clear and complete.** The pages table and the colour-coded key map are the best documentation here **[tested]**. In the browser, only HOME and EDIT have keyboard shortcuts among the page buttons, so trying other pages by keyboard is awkward **[code]**.

**Readability.** Measured on the 240 px screen **[tested]**: pages/play.png pixel scan.
- Knob labels are 9 px including descenders (about 7 px capitals), and resting values are 11 px.
- On a 1.3–1.5 inch panel that is about 1.0–1.6 mm, comfortable at 30 cm but hard at 70 cm standing.
- The value of the knob you're turning becomes larger and lit, which helps a lot **[tested]**: emu/02_cutoff_turn.png.

## 4. Layout and menu diving

The structure is flat: 12 pages on 8 buttons, never more than two presses deep, and a tap always brings you back to Play **[code]**. That is good for a 4-knob instrument. The cost is moves that need two hands, not depth.

**The ten things I'd reach for most in a long piece, counted from the Play page:**

| # | Control | From Play | Back to Play | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Drone pitch I / II | turn K1 / K3 | — | 60 ¢ a click; exact notes need SEL held **[tested]** |
| 2 | Cutoff I / II | turn K2 / K4 | — | about 1.2 semitones a click, no fine mode **[code]** |
| 3 | LFO rate | turn SELECT | — | always live **[tested]** |
| 4 | Overall movement | tap OCT− / OCT+ | — | 10 % a tap **[tested]** |
| 5 | Resonance | 1 tap (HOME) + turn | 1 tap | or hold HOME and turn to peek |
| 6 | Drone balance / level | 1 tap (HOME) + turn | 1 tap | no mute **[code]** |
| 7 | X-Wave (timbre) | 1 tap (EDIT) + turn | 1 tap | |
| 8 | Reverb / delay mix | 1 tap (FX) + turn K3 / K4 | 1 tap | |
| 9 | Filter LFO depth | 1 tap (LFO) + turn | 1 tap | black keys change layer while you're there |
| 10 | Swell rate / depth | hold ARP + turn | let go | two hands, can't stay **[tested]** |
| — | Sub octave, partner, HP | 1 key tap | — | only on the normal layer: from LFO pages, leave first **[code]** |
| — | Scene recall | 1 key tap | — | over the Scene time **[code]** |
| — | Feedback, Character, Reverb type | 2 taps (FX FX) + turn | 1 tap | |

**Pages that earn their place:** Play, Play 2, Osc, LFO, Partner, Effects. **Pages that don't hang together:** LFO 2, Tone and Effects 2 (see section 5).

## 5. Placement and information architecture

| What | Where now | Problem | Where it should go |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pulse width | Tone (ENV), K2 / K4 | it only matters near square, which is set on Osc. The DS-2 puts PWM beside X-WAVE | Osc page, beside X-Wave |
| Osc mix (sub level) | Osc (EDIT) | it sets weight, not waveform | Tone, beside Drive ("weight and grit") **[opinion]** |
| Cross-mod amount | LFO 2, K3 | its on/off is a key and it's a link between the drones, not an LFO setting | with the links. The Partner page could become "Partner & links" **[opinion]** |
| Delay LFO | wave on LFO-layer key 10, depth on LFO 2 K4, time on FX | split over three places | depth on Effects 2. The routing key can stay on the LFO layer |
| "Mod by" | LFO 2, K2 | a 3-way switch on a knob | fine on a knob, but rename it "Rate mod by" **[opinion]** |
| Speaker (low cut) | Effects 2 | it's about the room, but saved and recalled with every scene **[tested]** | Setup, kept out of scenes |
| Tune, Pitch lock | Setup 2, key 3 | also recalled by scenes **[code]** | keep out of scenes |
| Pitch lock, LFO reset keys | inside drone I's key groups | global controls look like drone I's | the lone key (11) or a group of their own **[opinion]** |
| Band-pass filter | nowhere | in the engine but not reachable | make the HP key cycle LP → BP → HP, like the sub key |
| Per-drone fade | nowhere | the engine supports it | e.g. hold PLAY + K1 / K3, or SEL + PLAY **[opinion]** |
| Feedback | Effects 2 | one of the most expressive controls, two taps deep | consider swapping it with Reverb type, so Effects shows the four mixes and Effects 2 holds type, speaker, character **[opinion]** |

**Duplicates:** none in function, only the "Width" name. **Not worth a page on its own:** Swell (hold-only) is fine as it is if it can also be latched.

## 6. Sound (brief)

- **Default sound:** D3 and A3 with subs one octave down, a gentle filter breath, and a Hall reverb at 25 % **[tested]**: default.wav.
  - The loudest part of it is the 73 Hz sub.
  - 47 % of the energy is below 200 Hz, so out of the box on the small speaker much of what makes it sound full won't be heard.
  - Speaker = 240 Hz costs 4 dB overall and 10 dB on the root **[tested]**: spk_on.wav.
- **Subsonic content:** at 27 Hz with the −2 octave sub, the strongest partial is 6.7 Hz and 59 % of the energy is under 20 Hz. With the ⅕ sub it's 5.3 Hz and 90 % **[tested]**: lowend_m2.wav, lowend_fifth.wav.
  - That energy is inaudible, takes up headroom and moves the speaker cone.
  - The DS-2 can do this too, but as an analog filtered square. A fixed high-pass around 20 Hz at the output costs nothing musically.
- **Switch changes:** no clicks with the default Morph or with Morph at its minimum. Sub, HP, partner, sync, link and cross-mod all changed without a burst of high-frequency energy **[tested]**: switches*.wav.
- **MASTER:** behaves as described. From 80 % to 100 % the RMS rises 0.8 dB and the peaks drop from −0.4 to −4.2 dBFS, so it saturates rather than getting louder **[tested]**.
- **Swell default:** about 2 Hz (period 0.49 s) **[tested]**. For a control called "Swell", a default of a few seconds would be more welcoming **[opinion]**.
- **The slowest LFO is 0.02 Hz (a 50 s cycle)** **[code]**. Long-form players often want minutes; scene morphs and the motion loop partly cover this **[opinion]**.
- **Cutoff resolution:** 0.098 octave per step **[code]**. With the resonance self-oscillating you can't tune it to a harmonic precisely; SEL fine-tune should work on cutoff too.
- **Stereo:** L/R correlation is 0.53 by default, which gives real width on headphones; the side signal is −5 dB, so there's little risk of cancelling in mono **[tested]**.

## 7. Suggestions

| # | Problem | Change | Cost | Priority | Owner decision? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Can't set pure or exact intervals between drones | Pitch link gap and pitch knobs step through intervals (just ratios when Tuning = just) while a modifier is held, or SEL + white key sets a drone to that note | medium | high | **yes**: which gesture |
| 2 | SAVE / scene store silences audio while playing | Fade out and in around the write, or keep scenes in memory and write only when stopped, with a "not yet saved" dot | medium | high | **yes**: delay the write, or allow a dip |
| 3 | Scenes recall Speaker, Tune, Pitch lock | Leave room settings out of scenes, as Fade already is | small | high | no |
| 4 | Subsonic energy | Fixed high-pass at ~20 Hz on the output, or fold subs below 20 Hz up an octave | small | high | no |
| 5 | Band-pass unreachable, README promises it | HP key cycles LP → BP → HP | small | medium | no |
| 6 | Swell page hold-only | Hold ARP to peek as now; tap ARP twice quickly (or SEL + ARP) to stay | small | medium | **yes** |
| 7 | No per-drone fade | Use the existing per-drone fade: e.g. SEL + PLAY fades drone II only | small | medium | **yes**: which gesture |
| 8 | HOME on Play jumps to Play 2 | HOME always goes to Play; Play 2 on a second tap within a short time, or HOME held | small | medium | **yes** |
| 9 | Pulse width away from X-Wave; LFO 2 a grab-bag | Osc = X-Wave + Width; Tone = Drive + Osc mix; cross-mod amount to Partner; delay LFO depth to Effects 2 | medium | medium | **yes** |
| 10 | Two "Width" labels | Rename to "PW" and "Stereo" | small | medium | no |
| 11 | Cutoff too coarse for a self-oscillating filter | SEL held also makes cutoff (and resonance) fine | small | medium | no |
| 12 | Global keys inside drone I's groups | Move Pitch lock / LFO reset to their own group, or redraw the guide's boxes | small | low | **yes** |
| 13 | OCT− / OCT+ names clash | Keep Movement (it's a good macro), but print the meaning on the screen's first boot and in the top bar; consider SEL + OCT = octave of drone I | small | low | **yes** |
| 14 | Small text when standing | Larger Play-page value font, or a "stage" view with only the four values large | medium | low | **yes** |
| 15 | Swell default 2 Hz; LFO slowest 50 s | Default Swell slower; extend the LFO's slowest rate to several minutes | small | low | **yes** |
| 16 | Default sound mostly under 200 Hz on the speaker | A first-boot or headphone-detect Speaker setting | small | low | **yes** |
